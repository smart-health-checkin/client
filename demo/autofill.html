Allergy review

This page plays the provider's data-collection app. Instead of asking you to type your allergy list from memory, it asks your health app for it — one tap, like autofill. Shared records usually carry the allergen and not much else, so the form then asks you only for what's missing: which symptoms each one causes, and how bad they get.
